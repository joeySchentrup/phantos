# Introduction (Post-Tri-War Era Revision)

The following document provides a geopolitical snapshot of the continent of Hurly in the aftermath of two cataclysmic conflicts: the Great Hurlen War and the Tri-War. Once a land of proud kingdoms and independent federations, Hurly now finds itself largely subdued and fragmented under the dominion of the Kobold Empire, also known as the Kledgelony.

Three years have passed since the end of the Tri-War—a brutal, three-front struggle between:

- The Kobold Empire, whose snawk-empowered metabolds and divine mandate from Rokesh laid waste to entire coalitions.

- The Twilight Throne, led by Erosia and her Twili and Husk legions, emerging from the Twilight Realm through shattered seals and ancient rites.

- The remnants of the Hurlen Federation, primarily based in the frozen bastion of Rus, struggling to hold together scattered alliances in the north.

Though Erosia was ultimately subdued—her armies dissolved and her form bound in mortal penance—the cost to the Federation was total. The signing of the Treaty of Silver Ash in the broken Hall of Unity at Hyperion marked the official end of Hurley’s independence. From this shattered peace rose a new political order.

Today, nearly every state on the continent exists as a protectorate of the Kobold Empire, whether through direct governance, puppet rulers, or military occupation. The once-proud flags of the Federation fly now only in ceremony, a hollow echo of sovereignty. Even Hyperion, the Eternal City, now serves more as a cultural showcase for Imperial diplomacy than a symbol of free peoples.

This document catalogues the current political, geographical, and historical states of the nations of Hurly as they exist under this new, imperial regime.

## Name: Alachua (Protectorate of the Kobold Empire)

Former Title: Kingdom of Alachua\
Current Government: Imperial Vassal-State under Kobold Imperial Governance\
Nominal Ruler: King Roland Madwell (Puppet Monarch)\
Imperial Authority: Overseen by Governor-Viceroy Chakruz, of the Kledgelony

Geography:\
Located in the southwestern-most region of Hurly, Alachua borders the Slaver’s Sea to the east, the Bay of Bliss and the Blood Sea to the south, the country of Mongues to the north, and Pomera to the east. Crisscrossed by rivers flowing down from the Monguian Mountain Range, its soil remains some of the most fertile on the continent—almost entirely arable, allowing for ease of transportation, irrigation, and agricultural exploitation.

Post-War Description:\
Once hailed as the “breadbasket of Hurly,” Alachua now serves as the granary province for the Kobold Empire’s Hurlen territories. Though the Madwell clan retains their title, their autonomy is nonexistent—the king's court court reduced to a ceremonial presence beneath the shadow of Governor-Viceroy Chakruz, a stone-armored Metabold appointed directly by the Kledgelony. Chakruz resides in Port Yvander’s Embrace, a newly-constructed Kobold naval port and shipyard that looms over the capital city of Marshentide.

King Roland, son of King Bors, once reviled for betraying the Southern Alliance during the Great War, is now viewed by his people as a toothless collaborator—a remnant of the old order who has bartered Alachua’s sovereignty for survival. The Madwell clan continues to manage internal trade and food distribution, but all exports, including grain, riverfruit, and livestock, are now subject to Imperial requisition quotas.

## Name: Bliss (The Isle of Bliss, Imperial Leisure Colony)

Former Title: The Council of Comfort\
Current Government: Imperial Chartered Colony under Kobold oversight\
Nominal Ruler: Chief Councilor Althidon Yllaran (Elven Noble)\
Imperial Authority: Overseen by the Imperial Ministry of Wellbeing and Culture, headquartered in Port Adoré

Geography:\
Located south of Hurly between the Bay of Bliss and the Blood Sea, the Isle of Bliss remains a tropical paradise, blessed with warm breezes, steady rainfall, and pristine coral-ringed beaches. Though untouched by major conflict during the Tri-War, its strategic location and opulent infrastructure made it a prime target for post-war acquisition.

Post-War Description:\
The Isle of Bliss was formally annexed by the Kobold Empire shortly after the signing of the Treaty of Silver Ash and rebranded as an Imperial Leisure Colony—a luxury destination reserved for high-ranking officials, foreign dignitaries, and loyal nobles. While its elven ruling council technically remains in place, all meaningful power lies with the Imperial Ministry of Wellbeing and Culture, a Kobold bureaucracy responsible for maintaining order, aesthetics, and "spiritual morale" among visiting imperial elites.

Chief Councilor Althidon Yllaran, an elven aristocrat known for his charm and complacency, remains the island’s figurehead, frequently seen hosting banquets and public rituals that glorify the union between the Kobold Empire and the peoples of Hurley. Though admired by tourists, he is quietly resented by many native Blissites, who now live under strict zoning regulations that restrict movement and commerce in favor of maintaining "visual harmony."

## Name: Celeste (Grand Celestial Magocracy, Protectorate of the Kobold Empire)

Former Title: Grand Celestial Magocracy\
Current Government: *Imperial Arcane Protectorate* with semi-autonomous governance\
Nominal Ruler: Grand Magus Enexeor (Imperial Liaison to the Arcane Council)\
Imperial Authority: The Imperial Ministry of Arcane Affairs, advised by the Twilight Concord Commission

Geography:\
Celeste lies nestled between the Swedcele Mountain Range and the regions of Swedanetil, Monteforte, and Napolei. Its plains, woven with powerful ley lines, are rich in ambient magic and home to a tradition of mystical inquiry dating back to the Drynell era. The capital, Mysthalis, remains a radiant city of floating towers, mirrored domes, and eternal auroras.

Twili Occupation and Harmonization:\
Celeste was among the first territories overtaken by the Twilight Throne during the Tri-War, drawn to the nation's proximity to leylines and its affinity for the Arcane and Light—both central to Erosia’s vision of balance through shadow. The Twili occupation was not marked by ruin but by ritual transformation.

- Public magic was reoriented toward harmonizing dualities—light and dark, life and memory, self and shadow.

- Several mage schools were adapted into Twilight Conservatories, where students explored non-linear spellcasting and dreamweaving under Twili tutors.

- Though controversial, the period is remembered by some as a time of spiritual awakening, especially among half-elven and gnomish populations.

Post-War Transition:\
With Erosia’s defeat and retreat into penance, Celeste was not destroyed or purged. Instead, the Kobold Empire negotiated a peaceful reclamation, recognizing the city's strategic value and cultural resilience. The Treaty of the Prism Court allowed for a dual-framework government:

- Grand Magus Enexeor remains in place, though his authority is shared with both Kobold overseers and a Twili delegation—now formalized as the Twilight Concord Commission.

- The city’s wards were rewritten using a hybrid schema: arcane glyphs layered with Kobold geomantic runes and Twili dream-sigils.

Arcane Culture Today:

- Public spellcasting is regulated but not suppressed. Magi must register schools of magic and pass a harmonic resonance test to avoid spell instability.

- Dark-aligned schools, such as necromancy and shadow magic, are permitted for research under licensed supervision—though closely monitored.

- Enchantments and constructs from the Twilight Era are catalogued rather than destroyed, and some have been incorporated into Celeste’s infrastructure.

- The city is now home to the Prism Forum, a diplomatic and academic institute where Kobold geomancers, Twili dreamweavers, and Celestian arcanists collaborate under imperial watch.

Public Sentiment:

- Some elder mages mourn the loss of pure arcane independence, fearing Celeste is becoming a puppet of larger metaphysical powers.

- Younger spellcasters embrace the new fusion of traditions, calling it the “Third Enlightenment.”

- Underground networks like the Starborn Circle still promote restoration of Celeste’s full sovereignty, though they advocate reformation, not rebellion.

## Name: Claire Fontaine (The Grand Duchy of Claire Fontaine, Kobold Imperial Partner-State)

Former Title: The Grand Duchy of Claire Fontaine\
Current Government: *Autonomous Client State* with integrated Kobold command presence\
Ruler: Grand Duchess Claire Fontaine (recognized as First Citizen of the Southern Accord)\
Imperial Affiliation: Signatory of the Treaty of Embervale, bound to the Kobold Empire by alliance and shared development charter

Geography:\
Nestled between Nabatea, Napolei, Mongues, and the Slaver’s Sea, Claire Fontaine is a mountainous country defined by the mineral-rich slopes of the Southern Monguian Range. Though small in size, its wealth in precious ores and arcane metals made it a strategic objective early in the Great War. Its fortresses overlook key trade routes and natural mountain passes to the continent’s core.

Early Alliance and Integration:\
Claire Fontaine was among the first nations to sign a cooperation pact with the Kobold Empire, largely due to Grand Duchess Claire’s pragmatic leadership and personal admiration for Kobold governance. She famously declared:

> “If the world must change, let us be among those who guide its shaping—not those who are shaped by it.”

Following the signing of the Treaty of Embervale, Kobold engineers and Metabold earthshapers were invited to assist in rebuilding war-torn areas and reactivating dormant mines. The result was a hybrid infrastructure model: Kobold stonecraft powering Fontainean industry, with human-Kobold guilds sharing management of factories and forge-chapels.

Societal Transformation:

- The duchy has become a model of cultural cooperation, where mixed-race communities—humans, Kobolds, and even Twili artisans—live in relative harmony.

- Claire Fontaine now hosts the Academy of Practical Concord, where Kobold and Fontainean scholars teach joint curricula on mineral alchemy, civic engineering, and draconic ethics.

- Citizens are granted access to Kobold trade privileges, and many serve in the Imperial Auxiliary Corps, a prestigious military-engineering unit.

Challenges and Conflicts:\
Despite its official status as a partner-state, some critics see Claire Fontaine as a vassal in velvet, citing:

- The increasing presence of Imperial Administrative Auditors, who have begun reviewing local taxation and education policies.

- The formation of the Civic Harmony Tribunal, a Kobold-supervised agency with the authority to overrule local laws deemed “counter-imperial.”

- A rise in rural discontent, particularly among old Napoleian families and former revolutionaries, who view Claire as a “traitor to the dream of independence.”

The Duchess and the Dream:\
Grand Duchess Claire remains widely beloved by her people for raising living standards and preserving dignity in the face of conquest. Yet in recent speeches, her tone has shifted:

> “We walked the road of peace, and peace is precious. But I wonder—if the path is only wide enough for one kind of step… is it truly shared?”

## Name: Corsica (The Province of Corsica, Imperial Reclamation Zone)

Former Title: Kingdom of Corsica → The Twilight March\
Current Government: *Kobold Imperial Protectorate* under military reconstruction oversight\
Nominal Ruler: King Hamon (Restored Monarch with ceremonial power)\
Imperial Authority: Administered by General Varnok of the 7th Stone Legion, reporting to the Imperial Office of Post-Twilight Recovery

Geography:\
Corsica occupies a vast stretch of the Hurlen Plains, bordered by Uterect and Fuchs to the north, Zorat and Plana to the south, Ismakale to the west, and the Coastal Sea to the east. Once considered the great plains of central Hurly, Corsica is now a scarred land dotted with burned-out strongholds, unstable ley fields, and shadow-blasted hollows.

### Phase I: The War-Torn Heart

During the Great War and Tri-War, Corsica became the blood-soaked heart of the conflict—its flat terrain perfect for sieges, cavalry, and artillery. It was the site of:

- The White River Campaign, which left the river poisoned and filled with corpses.

- The Siege of Albrançe, where half the capital was leveled in a three-week bombardment.

- And the infamous Nightfall Pact, when several Corsican commanders defected to the Twili rather than allow a Kobold incursion.

### Phase II: The Twilight March

After a year of battle, Corsica fell to Twili control and was restructured as the Twilight March, a semi-autonomous territory under the direct rule of Governor-General Tel’Vareth. During this phase:

- Public rituals of equilibrium and remembrance were instituted, and shade temples were erected where palaces once stood.

- A policy of “soft stillness” dominated civic life: dream-magic integrated into governance, curfews marked by phases of moonlight.

Many Corsicans were bewildered by the quiet, others swayed by its discipline. Yet some began aiding Kobold-aligned agents and guerrilla groups, leading to periodic border skirmishes with Twili outposts along the Plana front.

### Phase III: Kobold Reclamation and Protectorate Status

Following Erosia’s disappearance and the signing of the Treaty of Silver Ash, Twili forces were ordered to retreat to the Twilight Threshold, and Corsica was peacefully handed over to the Kobold Empire.

Corsica now functions as a Kobold Protectorate under military administration, branded a “Reclamation Zone” due to the lingering arcane corruption and its unstable infrastructure.

Key features of the current regime:

- King Hamon was restored to his throne—but only ceremonially. Real authority lies with General Varnok, a grizzled Metabold warlord overseeing the province’s “restabilization.”

- The Twili moon plazas and shadow monuments have been repurposed into Draconic fusion temples dedicated to the Dragons, and (controversially) Erosia as “the Penitent Night.”

- Former Twili collaborators have been allowed to remain, but only after undergoing oath-bonding ceremonies overseen by the Kledgelony.

### Current Status: A Land Between Loyalties

Corsica is a land of competing memories and layered allegiances:

- Kobold loyalists praise the infrastructure improvements and economic aid from Korland.

- Twili cultural loyalists, especially in the south, maintain small “Still Circles” that keep alive Erosia’s vision in hidden groves and former temples.

- Corsican nationalists, both old-guard monarchists and younger rebels, chafe at the foreign presence—whether it be stone-scaled or shadow-veiled.

A resistance movement, the Circle of the Unseen Dawn, is rumored to be forming in the ruins of the White River Pass. Their symbol—a crown of starlight behind a broken anvil—has begun appearing in burned fields and on garrison walls.

## Name: Ete (The Grand Etian Technocracy, Imperial Innovation Partner-State)

Former Title: The Grand Etian Technocracy\
Current Government: *Imperial Cooperative Territory* governed by the Etian-Kobold Innovation Accord\
Nominal Head: Grand Innovator Binks (Imperial Liaison Technarch)\
Imperial Authority: Guided jointly by the Imperial Ministry of Advancement and the Council of Ten

Geography:\
Situated south of Plana, west of the Papacy and Lakose, and north of Ishmakale, Ete is a nation woven from green-glass towers, forest-cradled workshops, and leyline-reactive soil. Its southeastern lowlands, rich in alchemical moss and conductive minerals, make it one of the most naturally suited regions for magitechnical synthesis on the continent.

### Fall and Peaceful Integration

Ete’s leadership foresaw the devastation that prolonged resistance could bring. With few standing armies and most of its energy invested in invention, Ete chose diplomacy. Within three months of the Tri-War, the Council of Ten opened negotiations with the Kobold Empire, leading to the swift signing of the Treaty of Etian Accord.

Rather than occupy or dominate, the Kobold delegation—led by Emissary-Architect Valkuraz—proposed an alliance built on shared genius and mutual uplift.

> “You have the mind. We have the stone. Let us build not a war machine, but a future.”

### Post-War Structure: The Innovation Accord

Under the Etian-Kobold Innovation Accord, Ete retained its identity as a technocracy, with the Grand Innovator still elected by the Council of Ten. However, a permanent delegation of Kobold engineers, geomancers, and metabold scholars now work alongside Etian innovators in cross-discipline think-tanks and collaborative design halls.

The capital, Ferrovale, has become a symbol of synthesis:

- Its central forum, once the Forge of Minds, is now called the Hall of Joined Sparks, where innovations are openly shared and patented by dual seals.

- The Elemental Conduit Network, a pre-war Etian dream project, was finally completed with Kobold stone-weaving—granting most rural citizens steady access to power and potable water.

### Social Transformation

Far from being subjugated, most Eteans describe the transition as a renaissance:

- Etian youth now travel to Korland’s College of Applied Stone on fellowship.

- Kobold earth-masons and Etian lenswrights have co-developed crystal-armored farming golems that have doubled food output.

- Festivals like the Festival of Sparks now celebrate Rokesh and Quintara Lotus jointly as patrons of creative order.

Even so, not all is harmonious:

- Some older guildsmen resent the new licensing protocols introduced by the Empire.

- A few inventors quietly protest “patent-sharing clauses”, which they see as thinly veiled nationalization.

- A whisper network called the Fractaline Chain still exists, not as a rebellion—but as a watchdog collective to ensure the Accord remains balanced and not one-sided.

### Current Status: Province of Partnership and Promise

Ete today stands as a beacon of peaceful transformation, celebrated by the Empire as proof that unity need not mean erasure. In fact, many now refer to the nation as the "Jewel of Accord"—a rare place where magic, machinery, and mutual respect converge.

> “If conquest is the only path, we shall innovate conquest itself—into collaboration.”\
> – Grand Innovator Binks, at the opening of the Hall of Joined Sparks

## Name: Fuchs (The Order of Fuchs, Imperial Shield-Province)

Former Title: The Order of Fuchs\
Current Government: *Military Vassal-State* under Imperial Command Integration\
Ruler: Grand Knight Vladimir Stalin (Imperial Warden of the Northern Bastion)\
Imperial Affiliation: Bound by the Oath of Steeled Brotherhood alongside Rus

Geography:\
Fuchs rests in the rugged foothills between Rus to the northeast, Hossari to the west, and Utrecht to the south. Defined by frigid winds, pine-thick highlands, and the lower stretches of the Rusan Mountains, it is a land hostile to invaders, but sacred to its warriors.

### During the Tri-War: The Shield That Did Not Break

Fuchs gained near-mythic status during the war as one of the only territories never fully conquered—by Twili, Federation remnants, or Kobold alike. Though nominally a vassal of Rus, it acted with total military autonomy, becoming the northern bulwark of resistance throughout the conflict.

- Fuchsian knight-companies held the Line of Fireglass Pass for over two years against Erosian spectral cavalry.

- The Siege of Stillhorn Hold, led by a combined Twili-Husk force, was repelled in one of the bloodiest battles of the war.

- Kobold legions never succeeded in breaching the Fuchsian heartland, even after Erosia’s retreat.

Many credited the region’s survival to its cultural discipline, terrain mastery, and intimate alliance with Rus—whose black-forged steel and runic arms gave the Order its edge.

### The Oath of Steeled Brotherhood

Though undefeated, the fall of the Twili and the collapse of the Federation forced a grim choice: isolation or subjugation.

In an act of solemn diplomacy, Grand Knight Vladimir Stalin and King Bors IX of Rus jointly signed the Oath of Steeled Brotherhood with the Kobold Empire—a compact of conditional vassalage, preserving internal autonomy in exchange for military allegiance.

Key provisions included:

- Fuchs retains full control of its knighthood and internal code, but now serves as an Imperial Northern Shieldwall against any future external threats.

- The Kobolds station a single General-Liaison in Fort Sternwatch, empowered only to coordinate joint campaigns—not command.

- Fuchsian forges are now granted Kobold-core ore reserves, enhancing their famed weaponcraft with geomantic reinforcement techniques.

### Cultural and Strategic Status

- Fuchs remains a proud, insular military order, revered for its uncompromising martial ethos and loyalty to its oaths.

- Kobold imperial agents refer to the Order as “The Immovable Edge.”

- The Knights of the Black Line, veterans of the Fireglass Campaign, now train elite mixed units of Metabold shock-troopers and Fuchsian lancers.

Still, not all within Fuchs embrace the accord. A faction of younger knights—known as the "Ironhowl Vow"—train in secret, believing that even proud oaths can rust when tied to foreign rule.

### Current Status: An Iron Wall with a Crown of Stone

Though no longer sovereign, Fuchs is far from conquered. Its place within the Empire is not that of a vassal begging favor, but of a shield demanded by necessity, respected for its unbroken valor and its deep bond with the stoic kingdom of Rus.

> “We did not fall. We bowed our blades—once. But should darkness rise again, we will stand first and last.”\
> – Grand Knight Vladimir Stalin

## Name: Hossari (The Queendom of Hossari, Twilight Stewardship Zone)

Former Title: The Queendom of Hossari\
Current Government: *Twili Autonomous Region* under Imperial Supervision\
Ruling Body: The Twilight Assembly of Hossari, advised by the Kobold Custodial Envoy\
Imperial Authority: Managed jointly by the Kledgelony Council on Shadow Affairs and the Imperial High Watch of the Breach

### Geography:

Situated along the northern coast of Hurly, bordered by Swedanetil to the west, Fuchs and Utrecht to the north, and Celeste to the southeast, Hossari is a land of frozen forests, silver lakes, and ancient ruins buried beneath moonlit snow.

At its core lies The Breach—a monumental rift between realms, where Erosia and her Twili legions reentered the world after millennia of banishment. The land around the Breach warps light and time, echoing with voices of the Twilight Realm that now bleeds faintly into the fabric of Hurly.

### Historical Significance: The Awakening of Erosia

It was in Hossari—beneath the ruined city of Vael-Thamor, once a center of elven orthodoxy—that Erosia was revived. Manipulated by grief-twisted spirits, the young mage Gillian Gearson broke the ancient seal, releasing not only the Queen of Dusk but the long-sealed gates of the Twilight Realm.

From this fissure emerged the Twili armies, forged from shadow and memory, the Husk Queen, twisted remnant of corrupted mortal souls, and a new ideology that sought balance through stillness, not annihilation.

### Post-War Governance: The Twilight Stewardship Zone

Following the war’s conclusion and the Treaty of Silver Ash, Hossari became the site of the most delicate compromise:

- The Twili were granted local self-governance, recognizing their sacred connection to the land and the Breach.

- In exchange, the Kobold Empire stationed permanent Custodial Regencies and constructed the Stone Halo Citadel, a vast fortress-temple encircling the Breach, meant to both protect and contain.

Today, Hossari is officially designated a Twilight Stewardship Zone—a land of permitted penance:

- The ancient Hossarian nobility was dissolved, and its former Queen Omylia disappeared—either exiled, imprisoned, or absorbed into the twilight.

- The Twili have established shadow-parliaments and revived old customs of memory-veneration and lunar communion.

- The Breach itself is encircled by five dream-spires, kept stable by joint Twili-Kobold arcanoengineering and watch-songs sung in both tongues.

### Current Life and Tensions

Hossari is a nation in transcendent limbo:

- Twili pilgrims arrive daily to kneel at the edge of the Breach, whispering prayers to Erosia, now remembered as "the Mourning Queen."

- Kobold engineers walk alongside Twili dreambinders to monitor and reinforce dimensional stabilizers around the breach perimeter.

- Ordinary Hossarians live in silence or suspicion—some adapting to Twili rule, others mourning the loss of identity, and a few secretly joining revivalist human sects that seek to reseal the Breach.

Despite the relative peace, Hossari remains volatile:

- The Breach pulses with increasing frequency during solstices.

- Rumors abound that not all who cross into the Twilight Realm return unchanged.

- A faction within the Twili Assembly—the Eclipsed Circle—has begun calling for full independence, rejecting both Kobold oversight and the memory of Erosia’s surrender.

### The Breach: The Wound That Watches

The Breach is more than a portal—it is a living threshold, a tear between day and dusk that hungers for meaning. Its glow shifts with the emotions of those nearby. Dreams become lucid. Voices whisper to those attuned.

Some believe Erosia herself dwells at its edge, her penitent form neither here nor there.

All agree: if it were to widen again, no wall—stone or shadow—could hold what might return.

## Name: Heraklion (The Imperial City of Heraklion, Heart of the Empire on Hurly)

Former Title: Hyperion, the Eternal City\
Current Government: *Direct Imperial Administration* by the Kobold High Command of Hurly\
Ruler: Governor-Imperial Sern Vokh, Voice of the Empress in the East\
Imperial Authority: Supreme Coordinating Seat of the Kobold Protectorates of Hurly

### Geography:

Located between Ishmakale and Pomera, Heraklion (formerly Hyperion) rests atop an ancient leyline convergence, surrounded by fertile plains and river-fed farmlands. Long considered the cultural and religious center of Hurly, it boasts towering monuments, libraries, shrines, and observatories said to have been founded by Quinn and Erosia during the Era of the Children.

### Fall of the Unconquered

For over ten thousand years, Hyperion remained unconquered—a neutral beacon of peace and knowledge throughout the rise and fall of empires. Even during the reigns of Drynell, the League Wars, and the First Hurlen Alliance, its walls never yielded.

That changed in the second year of the Tri-War.

The Kobold Empire, wielding metabold shock troops empowered by snawk venom, launched a precision assault known as the Siege of the Silent Steps. Within nine days, the Wards of Quinn were shattered, the city's sky-walls brought low by stone-shaping metabolds, and the Council of Eternal Harmony was dissolved.

The world was stunned. Hyperion had fallen.

### Post-War Transformation: The New Heart of Empire

Renamed Heraklion the city was chosen to become the supreme administrative center of the Kobold occupation of Hurly.

Today, Heraklion serves as:

- The headquarters of the Imperial High Command for Continental Affairs, coordinating policy, military actions, and cultural oversight across all protectorates.

- The residence of Governor-Imperial Sern Vokh, a veteran metabold general and personal appointee of Empress Joan.

- A spiritual capital, where the Temple of the Sevenfold Earth has been built over the ruins of the Old Unity Hall, honoring Rokesh and the Greater Dragons.

Its architecture reflects the union of old and new:

- Draconic basalt obelisks rise beside Hyperian marble colonnades.

- Floating rune-bridges hum above cobbled roads where priests and engineers walk together.

- Public amphitheaters stage not only plays but imperial lectures, celebrating the “Great Reconciliation of Hurly.”

### Current Role: Command and Compliance

From Heraklion, the Kobold Empire governs the continent with a blend of military precision, bureaucratic scale, and symbolic authority:

- All protectorate rulers must send envoys and reports to Heraklion quarterly.

- The Imperial Archive of Reconciliation stores censuses, magical logs, and philosophical revisions of Hurlyan history.

- A vast Geostone Array beneath the city enables rapid communication with Korland and all major provincial capitals.

Despite its splendor, Heraklion is not free of dissent. The Old Archivists, a shadow group of former Hyperian scholars, quietly distribute banned scrolls and attempt to preserve the pre-Imperial memory of the city.

Still, for most on the continent, Heraklion is the heart of the world, and its bells chime across the plains with the sound of peace… and power.

### Public Motto (Imperial Inscription):

> *“As stone shapes water and water carves stone, so shall Empire and wisdom endure as one.”*

## Name: Ishmakale (The Free State of Ishmakale, Reunified Imperial Protectorate)

Former Title: The Free State of Ishmakale\
Current Government: *Unified Kobold Protectorate* with transitional councils under imperial supervision\
Nominal Leader: *High Steward Zain of Rivermarch* (Imperial Regent)\
Imperial Authority: Overseen by the Southern-Continental Oversight Command, Heraklion-based

### Geography:

Ishmakale occupies the central heartland of Hurly, bordering nearly every major power. Its terrain ranges from the fertile southern riverlands to the Ashmire Forest and the Northern Mountain Range, making it a cultural and geographic crossroads. Once the powerhouse of the Hurlen Federation, it is now a land healing from division and war.

### The War and the Sundering

Ishmakale suffered more than almost any other state during the Tri-War. As both a military-industrial engine and a symbol of Hurlyan unity, it became a key objective for both Erosian and Kobold forces.

The region was eventually split in two:

- The North fell to Twili control, its cities transformed into sanctuaries of shadow-magic, ruled by local Assemblies of Stillness and overseen by the Twili Wardeness Caleyra.

- The South became a Kobold staging ground, its cities fortified with obsidian bastions and infused with geomantic infrastructure, governed by Metabold Marshal Drog Varnak.

A line of silence, marked by collapsed ley towers and scorched steppes, divided the nation.

Many thought Ishmakale would never be whole again.

### Reunification and Protectorate Status

With the fall of Erosia and the evacuation of most Twili command from the north, the Kobold Empire moved swiftly to secure and reunify Ishmakale—not through conquest, but through diplomacy and promises of reconstruction.

Under the Treaty of Ash-River Concord, the region was:

- Declared a single protectorate under Kobold stewardship.

- Appointed a transitional regency led by High Steward Zain of Rivermarch, a respected Ishmakali civic leader with ties to both factions.

- Given rebuilding priority status, with infrastructure projects led by both former Twili architects and Kobold earthshapers.

The Kobolds emphasized “healing over punishment,” and reestablished Ishmakale as a central transport hub for Hurly, rebuilding the old River-Tram Arteries and restoring key roads to Heraklion and Lakose.

### Current Status: A Wound in Recovery

Today, Ishmakale is a land of mixed memories:

- In the North, old Twili sanctuaries still function, and moonlight rituals are held under Kobold oversight. Some Twili settlers remain, recognized as minority communities.

- In the South, veteran legions now serve as civilian protectors, and Kobold-built forges operate side-by-side with rebuilt Ishmakali schools.

- Across the region, shrines have emerged to Rokesh, Agape, and Erosia—as symbols of memory, balance, and reconciliation.

Yet tensions remain. Border clans still whisper of secession, smuggling rings thrive in the ruins of the Ashmire, and youth on both sides argue over whose version of Ishmakale's future will prevail.

Still, for the first time since the War began, the bells of Mirascourt ring across both halves of the land.

> Proverb now common in Ishmakale:\
> *"Split once, mended twice—once by stone, once by shadow. Now we are stitched by both.”*

## Name: Kambot (The County of Kambot, Alchemical Protectorate of the Kobold Empire)

Former Title: The County of Kambot\
Current Government: *Imperial Alchemical Stewardship* under Count Kramor Kambot, with Kobold Scientific Oversight\
Nominal Ruler: Count Kramor Kambot\
Imperial Authority: Supervised by the Imperial Ministry of Biostrategic Resources out of Heraklion

### Geography:

Kambot lies on the eastern coast of Hurly, bordered by Zorat and Plana to the north, the Coastal Sea to the east, the River of Fate to the south, and the Papal States to the west. Its thick swamps, constant mists, and vine-strangled forests once earned it mockery across the continent—but also produced some of the most potent and rare medicinal herbs in all Hurly.

### During the War: Mud and Fire

Though not a major military power, Kambot became a crucial logistical battleground during the Tri-War. Its natural terrain was nearly impassable, which made it a buffer zone and a haven for guerilla warfare. The Twili briefly used its northern thickets as forward staging grounds, while Kobold forces seized the southern fens for alchemical resource gathering and venom processing.

Swedanetil is a land of eerie calm and cultural convergence:

- The native population has largely embraced the Twili, though some older nobles bristle at their influence over governance and spiritual rites.

- The Twili, in turn, see Swedanetil as a gift from Erosia’s sorrow—a place to mourn, remember, and renew.

- A fringe sect known as the Veilkeepers has begun blending Twili philosophy with Rokeshite stone-spirituality, seeking a unified twilight theology.

The Kobold Empire regards Swedanetil as a low-priority zone—stable, silent, and unlikely to rebel. But they watch it still, mindful that from silence, history often stirs.

> Twili Proverb now etched into the Sea-Gate of Swedanetil:\
> *“Here, the sun does not set. It sighs.”*

## Name: Utrecht (The Province of Utrecht, Imperial Frontier Protectorate)

Former Title: The Kingdom of Utrecht\
Current Government: *Imperial Administrative Territory* under civilian-military oversight\
Nominal Leader: Governor Almarek of the High Valley (Imperial-Appointed Marshal-Governor)\
Imperial Authority: Managed by the Office of Frontier Stabilization, based in Heraklion with a local seat in Bragholt

### Geography:

Utrecht lies along the high eastern ranges and low river valleys between Fuchs to the north and east, Napolei to the south, and Hossari to the west. A land of mist-laden ravines, frostbitten ridges, and ruin-laced border forests, Utrecht’s terrain is treacherous, beautiful, and brutal—an ideal ground for guerrilla warfare and prolonged resistance.

### During the War: Broken but Unyielding

At the start of the Tri-War, Utrecht resisted both Twili and Kobold advances, holding out longer than most neighboring states. Its mountain militias, known as the Winter Pike Companies, used terrain, hidden paths, and insurgent tactics to repel early offensives.

But after a year of pressure, Utrecht fell to the Twili, who:

- Converted key temples and court-houses into shadow-cloisters and memory sanctums.

- Imposed curfews enforced by Huskwalkers, sparking mass uprisings.

- Executed dozens of community leaders, fueling deep hatred and vengeance oaths.

Throughout occupation, rebel cells such as the Pale Lantern, Sons of Bragholt, and Valewatch conducted sabotage, assassinations, and information smuggling in coordination with Fuchs and Rus.

### Post-War Role: Cold Peace under Stone Hands

After the war, the Kobold Empire moved swiftly to dismantle the Twili apparatus and reassert control:

- The Pact of Emberlight, signed in the ruins of Bragholt, allowed Utrecht to enter the protectorate system in exchange for the removal of all remaining Twili influence.

- Kobold metabold patrols, backed by local fighters, rooted out Twilight sanctums still clinging to the ravines.

- Utrecht was reorganized into a frontier protectorate, with direct Imperial oversight but significant local input in military and infrastructure decisions.

Today, Utrecht is a tense but functioning territory:

- Governor Almarek, a former rebel commander turned imperial official, governs as a military marshal, balancing Kobold expectations with folk justice and loyalty networks.

- Infrastructure is being rebuilt—roads carved with Kobold runes, watchtowers built from stone and steel, and a new militia academy established in Bragholt.

- Twili symbols and rites remain outlawed—possession of dusk-weave or memory scrolls is punishable by exile.

### Current Status: Sober, Scarred, and Vigilant

Utrecht today is a land of cautious acceptance:

- Most citizens prefer Kobold stability over the memory-haunted rule of the Twili.

- However, veterans of the rebellion remain influential, and the Empire walks carefully to avoid sparking new uprisings.

- The Reconstruction Guilds, part of the Kobold civil apparatus, are largely respected, especially for clearing mines, restoring bridges, and building public halls.

In the high valleys, people still whisper old songs, but they no longer carry blades when they sing.

> Utrechter Saying:\
> *“We lost to shadow. We bowed to stone. But we remember who we are.”*

## Name: Xeries (The Principality of Xeries, Imperial Resource Protectorate)

Former Title: The Forest Principality of Xeries\
Current Government: *Hereditary Principality* under economic protectorate status\
Ruler: Princess Yalara IX of House Thistledew\
Imperial Authority: Coordinated by the Imperial Ministry of Resource Oversight, with a station in Elderglen

### Geography:

Xeries lies in the southeastern woods of Hurly, nestled between Lakose to the north, Pomera to the west, and the Coastal Sea to the south and east. The Great Eastern Greenwood, also known as the Crescent Canopy, defines its geography—vast, misted forests filled with timber, rare herbs, and whispering spirits.

### During the War: Peace Through Partnership

Xeries was among the first to formally align with the Kobold Empire, signing the Treaty of Bark and Stone mere weeks after the Empire’s forces began their continental campaign.

- Princess Yalara IX, citing the nation's limited military and deeply spiritual connection to nature, sought to protect her forests and people through alliance rather than resistance.

- In return for sovereignty and spiritual protection, Xeries agreed to become a primary lumber supplier, harvesting and refining the dense elderwood and flame-resistant pine needed for siege engines, shipbuilding, and fortifications.

While other nations burned or bled, Xeries remained untouched by invasion, serving instead as a quiet logistics lifeline for the Kobolds throughout the war.

### Post-War Role: The Empire’s Greenwood

After the war, Xeries maintained its protectorate status as a key natural resource partner:

- Timber quotas are determined annually by the Imperial Ministry of Resource Oversight, with strict ecological preservation clauses in place (honored more faithfully in Xeries than elsewhere).

- The capital, Elderglen, has become a hub of woodcraft, alchemy, and cultural export—notably in the form of spirit flutes, druidic carvings, and Kobold-inspired ceremonial masks.

- Princess Yalara IX continues to rule with quiet grace, beloved by her people for keeping the country safe and spiritually intact.

Kobold officials in Xeries are seen less as occupiers and more as accountants, ecologists, and trade partners—though some rural communities still resent the standardized harvest schedules and road expansions.

### Current Status: Quiet Prosperity, Watchful Growth

Xeries today is a land of serene stability:

- Its druidic circles, though carefully monitored, retain legal authority over sacred groves.

- The Forestguard, Xeries’ elite ranger company, now trains with Kobold terramancers to track illegal poachers and protect glade sanctuaries.

- A new class of merchant-rangers has arisen, bridging Kobold trade law with traditional Xerian ecology.

Though content, some worry that imperial reliance on Xerian wood will only increase—and with it, pressure to allow deeper cuts into protected forest zones.

> Xerian Proverb:\
> *“Better to offer the branch than have the roots scorched.”*

## Name: Zorat (The Territory of Zorat, Imperial Pacification Zone)

Former Title: The Warchiefdom of Zorat\
Current Government: *Direct Military Governance* under Imperial Martial Law\
Nominal Ruler: None — governed by Marshal-Governor Fraxin du Rochelaine, under mandate from Heraklion\
Imperial Authority: The Imperial Directorate of Internal Security and Stabilization maintains sole oversight

### Geography:

Zorat lies in the eastern lowlands of Hurly, bordered by Plana to the west, Utrecht to the north, Kambot and the Papal States to the south, and the Mountains of Rus to the northeast. It is a land of dense thickets, red clay plains, and scorched highlands, where long-forgotten ruins and crude fortress-towns dot the landscape.

### During the War: The Last Roar of the Federation

Zorat, long known for its unruly goblin-led tribal federations and warlord aristocracies, was among the final territories of the Federation to fall, holding out even after the fall of Hyperion and Celeste.

- Its infamous Ironspike Council, a loose assembly of tribal chieftains and war-generals, refused surrender—launching raids deep into Kobold-occupied territory from concealed earthworks and marshlands.

- Zorat was a thorn in the side of both Twili and Kobold forces, often switching sides mid-campaign and defying all expectations of coordinated governance.

- The final siege, known as the Ash Hunt, took three months and cost the Kobolds thousands of troops, largely due to the guerilla tactics and savage terrain.

When it finally fell, the Ironspike Council was executed, and the region was placed under permanent martial governance.

### Post-War Role: A Region Still at War

Officially, Zorat is a protectorate. In truth, it is a pacification zone—an occupied territory not yet civilized in the eyes of the Empire:

- Marshal-Governor Fraxin du Rochelaine, a metabold renowned for his ruthlessness, governs from Fort Rek’tar, an obsidian bastion raised over the former Ironspike Council hall.

- The region has been divided into resource zones and military districts, patrolled by Metabold legions and Kobold-trained dog-riders.

- Public gatherings are banned. Tribal customs are outlawed. Imperial law is enforced with stone and fire.

Despite this, the goblins of Zorat remain unbroken:

- Scattered remnants of the Ashfang Clans, once loyal to the Ironspike Council, now operate as mobile raiding bands, targeting supply lines, mining caravans, and reconstruction crews.

- These goblin warbands have adapted—learning Kobold tactics, stealing imperial equipment, and even hiring rogue Celestian mercenaries.

- A mysterious war-leader known only as “Red Scythe” is said to unite several clans in secret, aiming not to restore Zorat's government, but to burn imperial outposts one by one.

### Current Status: Suppressed but Smoldering

Zorat today is the Empire’s deepest wound:

- Trade caravans pass only under heavy escort.

- Most Kobolds posted to Zorat do not return, or return changed.

- Reconstruction is minimal, limited to fortified depots and watchtowers.

Even among loyal Kobold citizens, Zorat is spoken of in hushed tones—a place where the Empire may have won the war, but not the land.

> Saying etched into a Kobold helmet found outside Fort Rek’tar:\
> *“You can crush the stone, but you cannot tame the fire beneath it.”*
