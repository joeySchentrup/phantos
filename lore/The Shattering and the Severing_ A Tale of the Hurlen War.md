In the three-hundredth year of the Age of Concord, peace blanketed the continent of Hurly like the last snow of spring—fragile, fleeting, and illusory. For deep in the silver-cloaked forests of Hossari, a boy named **Gillian Gearson**, whose mind had been twisted by forest spirits and grief, unwittingly became the herald of calamity.

Driven by voices not her own, he uncovered an ancient seal buried in the ruins of a lost city of Hossari—once a city of dreams, now long reclaimed by vine and root. With hands slick from rust and magic, he broke the seal. And from the twilight maw rose **Erosia**, Twin of the Darkness, long ago banished to the shadow realm for her rebellion against her twin, **Quinn**, Lord of Day.

The seal shattered—and with it, the wall between realms. The Twili came first: eyes aglow with dusklight, clad in shadows made flesh. Then came the **Husks**, cursed remnants of the dead, twisted into servitude by the **Husk Queen**, their leader forged in agony. Hossari was the first to fall.

Yet this was not the only storm to stir.

On the southern shores, the **Kobold Empire**, guided by Empress Joan and her council of Metabold warlords—earthshapers empowered by ancient **snawk venom**—launched a mighty invasion. Backed by the fractured southern kingdoms and radical Rokesh cultists, they sought to expand their empire. The banner of Rokesh flew from the towers of Alachua and Pomera, and Kobold war-machines rolled northward, cracking the stones of the old Alliance roads.

Thus began the Tri-War—a three-headed fire:\
☼ The **Federation of Hurley**, noble yet divided, rallied behind the frozen hills of **Rus**.\
☾ The **Twili**, led by Erosia, sought to remake the world in endless dusk.\
⛰ The **Kobold Empire**, armored in stone and guided by divine love, marched for dominion.

The world wept. Cities like Nabatea and Heraklion shook beneath cannon and claw. Fields once golden were turned red, and no prayer rose unanswered—save those for peace.

But in Hurley’s darkest hour, hope stirred.

A party of champions rose from ash and ruin

With them walked the last hope of the divine dragons: the **New Draconic Champions**, children born not of blood, but of the six primal magics. These champions, as foretold by the ancient prophecy of Quint, were each destined to reflect the virtues of the original six dragons.

In a final confrontation beneath the roots of **Yggdrasil**, deep within the Lake of Mystery, the Avengers confronted **Erosia**. There, the Dragon of Night beheld her creation—chaos, suffering, shadow—and through the plea of the champions and the song of the heroes, **she remembered love**.

Erosia wept, and her sorrow unraveled the spell that bound the Twili and the Husks. Their armies dissolved like mist at dawn.

But peace had its price.

With Erosia’s fall, the balance shifted. The Federation, broken and weakened by its war with the Twili, could no longer resist the might of the Kobold war machine. Though the Empress Joan’s heart was heavy, her legions claimed victory. The **Treaty of Silver Ash** was signed in Hyperion beneath the broken dome of the Hall of Unity.

Hurley was not conquered.\
Hurley was *absorbed*.

Now, the continent remains a shadow of its former self—**a collection of client states**, each bowing in some way to the Kobold Empire. The Federation lives on, not in defiance, but as **a memory with flags**. Erosia wanders the world in penance, her form mortal, her power sealed.
