\[Excerpt from the musical ‘Heraklion’ (lines in italics are sung)\]

{Scene open on the camp of General Karl of Ismakale, just outside of Heraklion, Open flap of tent to reveal General Karl and his Commanders.}

**Scene A**

Karl: Well, we have made it. The city of splendor is just inside those walls, and soon it will be ours! *I’ve dreamt of this day since my childhood. The day I’d get to make a name for myself. I used to walk the halls of the greats of Ismakale...*

Commanders: *Doreen! Tared!*

K: *I looked to them and wanted to be just like them. They made this nation what it is and me who I am! And now I peer upon the walls of destiny.*

Coms: *The gates of fate!*

K: *They said they could fall.*

Coms: *Impenetrable!*

K: *They didn’t know I would come! For today is the day I make my name!*

Coms: *Karl!*

K: *We shall come at them with fire and sword!*

{Karl and Coms Exit tent and join rest of army drilling}

Army: *Fire! Sword!*

K: *We bring our cannons to take down the wall!*

A: *Boom!*

K: *And yet I cannot help but wonder how history will remember me... a valiant leader...*

Coms: *So cunning and brave…*

K: *a conqueror…*

Coms: *So powerful and strong…*

K: *what if something bad?... No, it will not be so. I cannot think of this no more, for today is the day that I make my name, a name I must choose. Men, make ready! We begin the assault! Ready, aim, Fire!*

{Cannon Fire, go to black}

**Scene B**

{Scene opens to Rosio praying at the shrine of light, explosions can be heard in the distance, a guard then rushes in frantically}

Guard: High Priestess! High Priestess! The Ismakalians are bombarding the walls! Please what should we do?

{Rosio stands up slowly with a smile on her face, begin music}

Rosio: *Why have you any fear? Don’t you know Ouro'ras is here? He has always protected us as his chosen people, thus we should not doubt him now. Those walls he built for us, the building and streets are his product too. So long as we have faith we shall not fall.* Come with me.

{They Exit the shrine and walk the streets}

Rosio: *Many have tried and many more have failed to take this city from us, but to no avail! The dragon of light saw to it that we should live in peace as a beacon to the world of the true way to prosper! So long as we stand no great evil will prevail! We are all happy here, we are the center of art and culture to this world! People come from all over just to know what we know! There is no safer place than here, we just have to keep the faith!*

{At end of song, a large explosion is heard and the sound of crumbling walls can be heard in the distance followed by the sounds of a marching army and screams, chaos, go to black}
