- C. 10000 BC- Erosian Wars, Erosia banished to Twilight Realm

- C. 9000 BC- Great Kobold exile

- C. 8500 BC- Kobold Tribesmen first arrive on the subcontinent of Korland aka Kobold Island, many Kobolds die dealing with the snawks

- C. 7500 BC- Majority of Kobolds on Korland are now immune to the negative effects of snawk venom

- C. 7000 BC- Kledgelony[^1] formed to help determine who is meta-capable

- C. 6500 BC- Snawks first tamed

- C. 6000 BC- Kledgelony[^2] becomes the de facto government on Korland

- C. 5000 BC- Kobolds on Korland grow to large enough numbers to form separate tribes

- C. 4000 BC- Kledgelony’s power begins to diminish and the local chieftains begin to take power

- (note: next ~3500 years are see relatively little in the way of notable changes in the Kobold’s status, but is mostly filled with a lot of in-fighting)

- C. 500 BC- Philip I of the Karling Clan unites the Kobold tribes for the first time, but empire is split after his death a short time later.

- 86-73 BC- Vercingetorix, a descendant of Philip I unites the warring Kobold tribes for good this time.

- 72 BC- Kledgelony declares Vercingetorix the Chosen Emperor[^3] of Rokesh and Ruler of all the Kobolds[^4].

- 72 BC - 5 AC- “Era of Great Expansion”[^5]

- 6 BC- Lanyaru submit to the Kobolds, accepting vassalage

- 1 AC- Chione born

- 3 AC- Scouts from the Dwarven Kingdom, Gnomish Republic, and Elven Confederacy “discover” Korland

- 4 AC- Start of First Kobold Wars

- 6 AC- Chione captured and brought to the Dwarven Kingdom as a civilian POW

- 17 AC- The treaty of Virandriel signed

- 26 AC- Start of Second Kobold Wars

- 57 AC- Napoleon, Emperor Vercingetorix’s second in command, is killed

- 58 AC- Treaty of Two Rivers signed, ending the Second Kobold Wars

- 61 AC- Vercingetorix abdicates the throne, his son Louis I, ascends to the throne

- 77 AC- Chione finds the city of Chiona[^6] at the confluence of the two Great Rivers

- 84 AC- Charles, brother of Emperor Louis I, marries Chione

- 86 AC- Birth of James, son of Chione

- 88 AC- Birth of Joan, daughter of Chione

- 96 AC- Vercingetorix the Great dies of old age

- 97 AC- Start of zombie apocalypse[^7], Kobold empire is relatively unaffected by the plague

- 101 AC- Great Rebellion led by the New Lanyaru Republic[^8]

- 156 AC- Emperor Louis I is assassinated without any heirs, after a succession crisis[^9], Joan, daughter of Chione succeeds to the throne.

- 158 AC- Treaty of Udoria[^10] is signed granting independence to all rebellious factions in the Empire and ending the rebellion

- 182 AC- Chione disappears, James, son of Chione, named new Chione, leader of the Chionittes, zombie apocalypse ends

- 203 AC- Neua discovered by Bobby Joe marking the beginning of the era of exploration

- 234 AC- Kobold explorer, Crait Kolm, explores Eastern coast of Neua and claims land for Empire

- 245 AC- Colonists from the Kobold Empire arrive and establish New Korland as a colonial territory of the Kobold Empire

- 267 AC- Ageless rebellion in mainlander part of Neua

- 270 AC- Great Meta purge

- 275 AC- Continent of Hurley discovered by Kobold Scouts, the decision is made to prepare for the invasion of the island

- 301 AC- Start of Hurlen War

- 304 AC - Erosia is reformed, the Hurlen War ends.

- 304 AC - The Kobold Empire assumes control over all of Hurley

[^1]: The Kledgelony were and still are a council of elders and religious leaders that fulfill several roles including leading religious gatherings, determining “worthiness”, record keeping, intermediaries and whatever odd jobs that were needed by the local population.

[^2]: The Kledgelony effectively acted as both the political and religious heads in the early years of the Kobold tribes, but once the tribes took stronger holds of their affairs, they were relegated to just the religious role and effectively acted as the only thing keeping the Kobold aggression in check from them massacring each other.

[^3]: The structure of the is a single head of state, the emperor, rules over the remaining population and beneath him are the Kolmar, sometimes referred to as governors and the local Kledgelonies. Even once Chionism began to spread in the Empire, the Kledgelonies still held vast amount of power on the local scale.

[^4]: With the rise in the empire, saw the return of the Kledelony’s power as the emperor’s power were bestowed upon him by the approval of the Kledgelony.

[^5]: This time saw the Kobolds expand west-ward to several small island chains and to the islands surrounding Korland, ironically this left their eastern side undefended when the Kobold Wars started allowing for several early victories by the “soft-skins”

[^6]: Chiona is effectively a city-state with fairly little lands to its name. The Dwelvan lands that separate the Dwarven Kingdom and Elven Confederacy are de facto under its control, but makes no claims to it so it does not upset its much larger neighbors.

[^7]: The zombie apocalypse also saw a large spike in Chionite followers as the religious group provided aid to those afflicted by the apocalypse drawing in massive amounts of converts.

[^8]: It should be noted that not all of those in the rebellious areas were sympathetic to the rebellion. The most influential loyalist group were the “rock” Lanyaru who sided with the Kobolds during the rebellion and remained citizens years after the rebellion ended.

[^9]: Charles was next in line for the throne, but he abdicated it since he wished not to leave his wife during the trying time they were in and many Kobolds wished not to James ascend to the throne since he was the heir apparent to his mother for the title of Chione.

[^10]: This treaty effectively reduced the Kobold Empire down to just Korland and its surrounding islands.
