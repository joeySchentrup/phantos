## **The Greater Dragon Pantheon of Hurly**

**Born of the Six Primal Dragons**, these 15 divine beings embody fusion elements—domains not of pure force, but of **balance, relationship, and transformation**. They are venerated across Hurly in various cults, shrines, and folk traditions.

###  **The Six Primal Dragons (The Divine Founders)**

- **Ouro’ras** – Dragon of **Light**, creator of humans and many sentient peoples.

- **Golestandt** – Dragon of **Darkness**, shaper of night creatures and Erosians.

- **Vlaurunga** – Dragon of **Fire**, mother of machines and metallic beings.

- **Yvander** – Dragon of **Ice**, mother of the seas and creatures of cold.

- **Quintara Lotus** – Dragon of **Arcane**, creator of magical beings and mysteries.

- **Rokesh** – Dragon of **Earth**, father of beasts, gems, and Kobolds.
