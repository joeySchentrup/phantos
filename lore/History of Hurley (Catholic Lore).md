- C. 10000 BC- Erosian Wars, Erosia banished to Twilight Realm

- C. 9500 BC- Founding of Drynell Empire, marking the second[^1] time the continent of Hurley was united under one government.

- C. 9100 BC- Establishment of the city-state of Nabatea, soon becomes the center of trade on the continent.

- C. 9000 BC- Faults begin to show in the strength of Drynell’ empire’s control, Emperor Zarkion IX blames the non-humans[^2], exiling them or enslaving them.

- C. 9300 BC- Great Rebellion, Emperor Ceasarious V declares that all races shall be intermixed so as to avoid further uprisings

- C. 8500 BC- Collapse of Drynell Empire

- C. 7000 BC- King Ottomus of Pomera conquerors all the Core region of Hurley and is crowned the new Holy Drynell Emperor[^3]

- C. 5000 BC- The HDE splinters[^4] and the country is now united in name only

- C. 4000 BC- The Dark Cult[^5] arrises

- C. 3000 BC- The Holy League forms between several southern countries[^6] who agree to split the land amongst themselves

- C. 2000 BC- King Falicitus is named the first king of the newly-formed nation of Ismakale inside the Core region.

- C. 1000 BC- The Holy League succeeds in its mission to unite the south among them

- C. 500 BC- Ismakale unites the remaining princes of the Core regions of the HDE

- 103 BC- The start of the League Wars[^7]

- 1 AC- The treaty of Heraklion[^8] is signed, bringing an end to the League Wars

- 33 AC- Ismakale brings down the Great Walls of Heraklion[^9] and occupies it becoming the first in history to do so.

- 89 AC- Several leaders of nations who used to belong to the Northern League[^10] begin talks of a new great Alliance

- 150 AC- The New Northern Alliance is formed uniting the Northern nations in a compact of defensive protection against other nations.

- 195 AC- The Northern Alliance spreads its membership to include the western nations[^11] and Celeste

- 223 AC- After losing to the Northern Alliance in a war of aggression, the nation of Ismakle join the Alliance[^12]

- 277 AC- All but the newly reformed Holy Leauge have joined the Hurlen Alliance

- 280 AC- The Second Leauge War begins

- 290 AC- The treaty of Homosassa is signed, ending the second Leauge War and forcing the entire continent into the Hurlen Alliance

- 301 AC- Hurlen War[^13]

- 304 AC - Erosia is reformed, the Hurlen War ends.

- 304 AC - The Kobold Empire assumes control over all of Hurley

[^1]: The first was under Erosia’s rule

[^2]: The Kobolds referred to this as the Great Exile and were forced to seek refuge elsewhere eventually ending up on the sub-continent of Korland. The Azveltarians were all but wiped out or had fled and the Porcs were enslaved.

[^3]: The Holy Drynell Empire, or HDE for short, was a much less impressive force on the world stage but did permit many small nobles to rise to become very powerful in the Core region such as the Ismakale state.

[^4]: This was primarily due to the practice of gavelkind succession laws

[^5]: The Dark Cult was a group of people who believed that Erosia needed to be brought back from the Twilight realm but knew not how, so they kept to the shadows and waited for a time to strike. Some believe that Gillian Gearson’s parents were actually members of this cult and place a spell on her a birth that linked her to Erosia eventually allowing for her return.

[^6]: Pomera, Alachua, Mongues, Lakose, Xeries

[^7]: Many speculate that the League Wars were started as a result of Ismakales’ growing power. The Wars saw a four-way war between the North, Holy, Ismakalian, and Nabatean Leagues

[^8]: As the only neutral city, Heraklion was the place that was designated for the signing of the peace treaty, many believe that not much was achieved by this treaty other than assuring that Hurley would require time to rebuild.

[^9]: It was originally believed that this was impossible, but Ismakale did it by using new technology

[^10]: Swedanetil, Hossari, Uterect, and Rus with its vassal, Fuchs.

[^11]: Corsica, Plana, Zorat, Ete, Papal State, Kambot

[^12]: Now renamed the Hurlen Alliance, the Northern Alliance moved the alliance capital to the newly freed city of Heraklion as a sign of unity among the continent.

[^13]: Three-way war between the Hurlen Alliance, the Kobold Empire, and the returned Erosian Empire.
