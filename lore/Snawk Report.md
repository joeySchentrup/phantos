SNAWK VENOM AND ITS USES AND APPLICATIONS WITH PARTICULAR INTEREST TO ITS PLACE IN MODERN IMPERIAL KOBOLD SOCIETY

ABSTRACT

A brief look at how the Snawk and, more importantly, it’s venom has impacted the modern society of the Kobold empire. This paper contains information on the history of the snawk venom in Kobold society and on the metabolic structure of the snawk venom and how it’s reaction allows the ‘victims’ to utilize the strange power of the venom.

HISTORY OF VENOM IN KOBOLD SOCIETY

From my research, I have discovered that the domestication and use of snawks in Kobold society dates back to ancient times much like the domestication of pot puppies and elemental were common in other parts of the world. This close relation between these creatures eventually led to some discoveries such as that some chosen few who were exposed to the venom and did not die eventually developed magical abilities allowing these Kobolds to manipulate the earth like an extension of their bodies.

This discovery led to a new hierarchy of Kobold society were only those with this ability were permitted to lead the Kobold tribes, and those with this ability were separated from those without. These higher Kobold were given the term of ‘Metabold’ to further widen and justify the divide.

Luckily, as the Kobolds grew more advanced, the system for determining who was compatible with the venom grew too. A religious caste who could ‘communicate’ with Rokesh was created, called the Kedgelony, and using that insight they more easily sorted out who could receive the power and who could not. They even developed new techniques that limited the amount of unfortunate reactions.

Then, however, the apocalypse happened. Snawk populations dropped and Chionite adherents grew in numbers. The demand for snawks grew so much and the supply so little that the Empress decided to hide the remaining snawks locked within hidden vaults only accessible by the Empress, the Kedgelony, the KSB, and the General staff. Where now only a select few are permitted the venom’s power.
