CHILDREN OF ROKESH

DESCRIPTORS: CULT

THREAT ASSESSMENT: HIGH

DESCRIPTION: Group of radicals who follow the traditional Kobold religion and are angry about recent Chionite influences in the Kobold Empire. They wish to restore the old ways and depose the current Empress, whose mother was Chione, in favor of Prince Louis, her cousin.

APPROPRIATE STRENGTH: 1% of Kobold population; 5% of nobility

RECOMMENDATION: Outlaw Rokesh worship and begin purging known members (Bolder method).

This report has been conducted and put together by Special Agent Mathalus of the Kobold Security Bureau’s Internal Affairs Commission for the eyes of the Kobold High General Council and Empress Joan only.
