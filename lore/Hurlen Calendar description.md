### **Hurlyan Calendar Overview**

**Year Length:**\
≈ **364.3314 days**

### **Structure of the Year**

- **15 Months**, each with **4 weeks**

- **Each week has 6 days**

- **Total standard days:** 15 months × 4 weeks × 6 days = **360 days**

- **+ 4 Intercalary Days** (not part of any week or month)

- **+ 0.3314 Corrective Day** annually (accumulated into a leap-day every ~3 years)

6.  **Golestday** – Dark (Golestandt)

### **Special Intercalary Days (Holy Days)**

Not counted as part of any week or month. These days are used for festivals, renewal, and divine worship:

1.  **Day of the Flame Crown** – Dedicated to **Kalistos**, the Creator God. Marks the **new year** with reflection and gratitude.

2.  **Day of the First Light** – Celebrates **Quint**, guardian of Day and joy. A festival of sunrise, laughter, and hope.

3.  **Day of the Deep Veil** – Honors **Erosia**, the shadowed Twin. A solemn twilight vigil and dream rite.

4.  **Day of the Breath Eternal** – Second holy day of **Kalistos**. Celebrated in midsummer as the "Sigh of Creation", focusing on meditation and cosmic harmony.

### **Corrective Days (Leap System)**

- Each year contains 364.3314 days.

- A **corrective "Kalistian Day"** is added roughly **every 3 years**, also dedicated to **Kalistos**.

- These are not named, but simply referred to as **“Day of Realignment.”**
