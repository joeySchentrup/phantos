# Electrum

## Electrum Shop

| Item | Price (in Electrum) | Description |
|---|---|---|
| One Gold | 5 | A piece of gold |
| Free level up | 40 + 10*(l!/[(l-2)!]) | Level up your character one level. l is equal to the level you are leveling up to. To calculate the cost, you can use the level up cost calculator tab. |
| Enhance a weapon | 100*l | Go from +0 to +1 or +1 to +2, etc. l is the level that you are upgrading to. |
| Learn a language | 250 | Learn any language |
| Power Stone | 1000 | Gain a power stone (pierre d'amour) |
| Know the location of anything | 2500 | Your character learns the location of anything in the game world. |
| Gain a Legendary Dragon Spell Scroll | 5000 | Gain a random Legendary Dragon Spell Scroll |
| Wish | 10000 | Cast the spell 'Wish' |
