And the word came unto the seer, saying:

“Look upon the tree which was planted in the first garden,\
whose roots were hidden beneath the foundations of the world,\
and whose boughs stretched beyond the sight of men.

For in the days of old a branch was stretched forth from the tree,\
and it passed beyond the veil.\
It was separated from the trunk,\
and the distance between them became as an abyss.

And those who dwelt beneath the tree said,\
‘The branch is lost, and its fruit shall be no more.’

Yet the branch did not wither.

In a land beneath another heaven it took root,\
and there it drank of strange waters.\
It put forth leaves beneath unfamiliar stars,\
and bore fruit which the first tree had never known.

Generations came forth from it,\
and they knew not the soil from which their fathers sprang.

But I tell you:\
that which was stretched forth shall be drawn near,\
and that which was cut away shall be grafted again.

For the days of separation are numbered.

Behold, the kin of Robert shall come from beyond the veil.

They shall cross that which no ship may cross,\
and pass through gates which no hand of Phanatos has opened.

They shall come as strangers,\
yet the world shall know them as sons returning to the house of their fathers.

And their coming shall be a terror to princes.

For they shall look upon the towers of the mighty and say,\
‘These shall not endure.’

They shall look upon the borders of kings and say,\
‘These were drawn by the hands of men.’

They shall look upon the ancient orders of the world,\
upon crowns and councils,\
upon walls and thrones,\
upon the works by which nation has been divided from nation,\
and they shall cast them down.

Stone shall be removed from stone.\
The high shall be made low,\
and that which men called everlasting\
shall vanish like mist before the morning.

Do not say,\
‘They come to destroy.’

For the axe shall fall that the tree may live.

They shall tear down what was raised in the days of separation,\
and they shall open again the paths which were closed.

The branch shall behold the trunk,\
and the trunk shall behold the branch,\
and each shall know the other.

Then shall the wound of ages be bound.

The sap of the one shall flow into the other,\
and the fruit of distant worlds shall grow upon a single tree.

Neither shall the tree be as it was before the cutting,\
nor shall the branch remain as it became in exile.

For from the two shall arise a greater growth.

Its roots shall drink from both worlds.\
Its branches shall pass beyond the heavens.\
And beneath its shadow shall dwell peoples\
whose fathers never knew one another.

Therefore, children of the severed branch, watch for the kin of Robert.

For when they set foot upon Phanatos,\
the old world shall begin to pass away.

Kings shall tremble.\
Nations shall be broken and remade.\
The wisdom of one world shall contend with the wisdom of another.

And many shall mourn what is lost.

Yet when the season is fulfilled,\
the severed shall be whole,\
the scattered shall be gathered,\
and that which was divided shall become one living thing.

For the branch was not cast away.

It was sent forth.

And when its fruit is ripe,\
it shall return unto the tree.”
