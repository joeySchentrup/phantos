Aleia of Hossari- Born to the king and queen of the royal city. The people adored her parents, because they had rid the city of elven magic, which was feared to the point of death. Aleia was born with a secret: she was schizophrenic. Her parents were terrified that someone might find out, so they were very hard on her to stay quiet. When she was very young, they gave her a wolf named Aeson to keep her safe, and he became her beloved friend and protector. Aleia spent endless hours in therapy, but one day, she persevered. Her parents thought the therapy was successful, but Aleia had never once attended. She had been practicing elven magic when she was supposed to be in “therapy”, and had entered a gamble to change her schizophrenic fate. She was secretly in training, every day, so that she could run away in search of a new life. Her trainer, Druindar, was one of the best sorcerers there had ever been. He came from a small clan of elves tucked away in the mountains above the city, and she travelled there every day with Aeson to learn magic. He knew that she had magical abilities that far surpassed anyone of her age, although she did not know it herself. Druindar taught her how to hone her natural magic, especially in protection and battle. She progressed far under his careful teaching, and came to be one of the greatest apprentices Druindar had ever seen, even though she was forced to shroud it in secrecy by fear of her parents. She knew that she was supposedly destined for royalty, but she was made for magic. Aleia lived in fear of her parents ever finding out, and planned to run away as soon as she finished her training. She loved her parents dearly, but she knew that she could never stay. In the middle of the night, once her training was complete, Aleia disappeared from the castle with Aeson by her side. She began her new life, travelling and practicing magic. She has won many battles, and saved many villages along the way, but is always on the move with Aeson. Aleia picked up a job from a rival plantation to salt the fields of Ediphus. When she snuck in one night to scope out the plantation and plan out her sabotage, she sensed magic coming from the mountainside, the same magic that she had been practicing for so many years. With the help of her wolf, Aeson, she found Gavlock to be the source of the magic. When she found him, he was seething with rage. Before approaching him, she sent Aeson to calm Gavlock. At first, the sight of the wolf puzzled him, but calmed him quickly once he nuzzled his leg. That’s when Aleia approached. Carefully, she introduced herself as an elven mage from Hossari, and asked why he was so angry. Gavlock began to tell her everything about Ediphus and his parents. Aleia took the opportunity to befriend the grieving elf. Then she explained her job and offered him half of the pay if he would help. Gavlock took advantage of the opportunity to get back at Ediphus, but with one condition: she would have to help free him from his bondage with Ediphus. Aleia agreed and concocted a plan. The night they salted the fields was to be the same night as the celebration of Ediphus’s birthday. That way, Gavlock could escape Ediphus’s anger when he found out about his ruined fields. Gavlock let Aleia into the back of the plantation house late at night, after Ediphus had been inebriated beyond the point of reason. That is when Aleia challenged him to a poker game. Being the card shark she was, she led Ediphus and the other attendants to pour their money into the pot. She lost the first few rounds willingly to let them gather confidence to put more in their pot. She needed Ediphus to bet Gavlock. By law Gavlock could not be set free, he had to be bought or won. By the fifth game, she let loose and destroyed everyone in attendance. Ediphus, being stubborn, tried to win his money back, but when he didn’t have any more to bet, he put Gavlock on the line. After revealing her cards and winning the last round, she had won both a significant amount of money and Gavlock, her new slave. They then salted the fields and fled into the night.

Gavlock- Born into slavery, and is why he doesn't have a last name. His parents were slaves tasked with harvesting Dragon Lilly for Ediphus, a wealthy plantation owner. The work was very difficult, so Gavlock being young worked in the plantation house with his mother as a butler. It was there he was trained in the utmost etiquette. He, for example, was trained to always address people by their full title.There was very little he knew about his family because his parents died when he was 6 years old. The memories he had were mostly of his mother. She would spend the night singing to him to calm him down after watching his parents getting beaten by the slave master. The last one he had was the night his mother turned 30. Ediphus was drunk from a party he held soley for his hedonistic desires. Gavlock was in the corner of the main hall while his mother cleaned. She knocked over a vase while Ediphus stumbled by. In his drunken stupor, he ordered the slave master to "put down" Gavlock's mother as she was clearly getting "too old". As the slave master dragged Gavlock's mother by the hair to the back of the house, he screamed in protest. Later joined the Avengers and was turned into a husk.

Gillian “Gilly” Gearson- Gilly was only a mere child when the armies came. She came from a peaceful, trusting, and quiet town called Xerion. She loved everyone in the village, and everyone loved her, especially her two parents, the engineers of the town. They built everything from small machinations that told the time, to grand metallic golems that were meant for gardening and building homes. Gilly loved her parents, and loved their practice. She loved it so much that she started helping along, and by the age of 15 she had already mastered her skills and become an engineer. Just in time for the invasion.See, Xerion was a part of a larger nation called Gautenia, one of the nations opposing the larger union. So they went to war. Xerion was forced to supply weaponry, turning the peace keeping golems into war machines. The greater union saw this as a key site to take, so they decided to invade it. Gillian’s parents, the heads of the golem war mechanization process, knew that their daughter had to be kept safe. She was the only one that knew their secrets, they techniques, and their love. So they sent her away on a trading convoy. The convoy was not merciful to stowaways though. So when they found her, they roughed her up and left her in the nearby forest, the Medullary Forest. There, she was forced to survive on her own. A few months passed, and she kept on remembering her parents, and all that they taught her. She would make tools, and machinery out of the woodland rocks and branches. The spirits of the forest were impressed. So they spoke to her. They spoke to her in many voices, urging her to trust them, slowly enticing her, using her want for knowledge and love. Eventually, they won her over, and they taught her many things relating to dark magic, especially in working with the dead. There in the forest, her magical abilities grew. However, the spirits took as much as they gave, and Gilly was left without any real thinking ability for herself. They did this so that she would always obey.She never forgot her parents though, she never forgot what they taught her: their secrets, their techniques, and their love. This love eventually overcame Gilly, and she left the forest in order to search for her parents, wherever they were in this new world. Years had passed, Gilly was not used to the new world. The aftermath of the rebellion was still present everywhere, and she would salvage some wreckage to make tools to use and sell. If she needed to fight, she would kill. That’s how the spirits taught her. She would go on many adventures and did anything anyone asked of her; although, the quest to find her parents was always priority, and she traveled the continent always looking.

Reina DeAyuda- Born in the rebel country of Pomera . At the young age of 8, war ravaged her homeland and her parents, two rural physicians, were taken to serve in the army. By age 10, Reina and her six brothers and sisters had become orphans. It became her duty to take care of all the children; not just her brothers and sisters but all the orphaned children of the village. Along with taking care of a dozen young children, Reina one day stumbled upon a creature, an injured, abandoned pot puppy. Mandrake, as she named him, grew to be strong, healing from the injuries of his past and developing a fierce loyalty for the large family. Reina, as head of her household, often hunted with the men of the village. Some accepted her but most scorned her, believing that the children she kept alive were taking resources from the other villagers. However some men admired her strength, and she soon developed strong friendships. One man, Santiago, began to help her with the children, and over time, he began to fall in love with the woman Reina was becoming. Reina, however, was more concerned with her growing group of children, as more parents died in the wars. Yet, life happens. Just as Reina began to open her heart and life to Santiago, he was called off to war. And just like that, Santiago was gone. Reina’s passion began to change; she wanted change. She placed her next oldest sister in charge of the children, and started her journey. She set off on the road with Mandrake and no plan; only carrying the anger of having her childhood, parents, and lover taken from her. Walking away from the village in the dead of night, she stopped by her parents graves to say goodbye one last time. Kneeling at her parents grave, she heard a rustling across the park and Mandrake began to bark. Taking out a small knife, she prepared to defend herself, when she saw a finger sticking out of the dirt. She walked closer, examining the wiggling finger. As she came closer, she realized that it was less of a finger and more of the remains of a finger. Now more curious than scared, she began to dig at the hand. The Skeleton began to wiggle, getting more and more excited as Reina dug towards the head. The skeleton, eventually settling on the name Scully, turned out to be a funny little misfit with no cares or friends in the world. Together,Reina, Scully and Mandrake set off, no direction or plans, only hoping to help restore order and stop the wars that had destroyed so much. Later joined the Avengers and was one of the ones who fell.

Roland Madwell of Alachua- A Berserker tall dwarf from the dissenting Kingdom of Alachuca. He was born into the Clan of Madwell, one of the more powerful and wealthier clans in the kingdom. The clan started as caravanners that transported supplies throughout the kingdom. They had extensive knowledge of all the trade routes in the kingdom and were the suppliers/transporters of goods for the Alachuian military.\
When the war with the Alliance broke out the clan worked closely with Alachuian military to ensure that the local trade routes where protected and to transport supplies to the front. At this time Roland was 15 years old and was helping his family to manage the different trade routes. He was trained by his family in combat to defend himself and anyone he was travelling with. On a supply run to a local village Roland was approached by two hooded figures that said they needed meet with his father to discuss business with him. Roland told them that the clan had no interest in new clients and told them to leave him alone. One of the figures grabbed Roland and told him that they were not asking. Thinking quickly Roland headbutted the hooded figure causing him to release Roland and revealing that the hooded figure was Porcish. Before Roland could attack again the other hooded figure told the Porcish that it was time for them to leave and threw a smoke bomb that allowed them to escape without a trace. Later that month he would see the pair again leaving his clan house. Roland ran inside to find his father and ask him what they wanted. His father told him that the pair were Alliance operatives and that they wanted him to stop running supplies to the troops in exchange for protecting the clan during the war and that they will be put in place as the new ruling family in Alachua after the Alliance takes over the region. Roland asked why they would make them the ruler family. Roland’s father, Bors, explained to Roland that the Madwells at one point in their history were part of the royal family. One of their ancestors was sent to battle with one the ancient kingdoms and taken captive. The family cold not pay the ransom and believed that he was killed. However, he was able to escape and find his way home but his crown was given to his brother. After learning about losing his crown he deiced to begin new life and started the clan of Madwell, waiting for the day that his descendants could take the crown. Bors told Roland that he plans on Roland going with the agents the next day to the Kingdom of Ishmakale to beginning his training and to represent Alachua in the Alliance. In the mean time Bors will comply with the Alliance’s demands and assist them in winning the war and rebuilding the kingdom. Roland knew that he no choice but to listen to his father and packed his things to leave the next day. The next day the agents came to take Roland Ishmakale where he would be educated and was trained to be a fighter. To protect him the Alliance put the Porish agent (Ron) as his bodyguard. The Ron went everywhere with Roland, with or without Roland permission. By time he was 20 he was made the Squire of Saphara the Supreme of Ishmakale. Roland used this position as an opportunity to learn more about the Alliance tactics and power struggles. During this time the Kingdom of Alachua was ruled by Bors but it was only a puppet government for the Alliance and his family had no true power in the land. Roland hopes that by working with Ishmakale to protect the Alliance that he could earn the Alliance’s trust and give his family more power in their kingdom and in Alliance politics. Later joined the Avengers and was one of the fallen.
