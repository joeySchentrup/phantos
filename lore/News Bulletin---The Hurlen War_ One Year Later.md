The Hurlen War: One Year Later

Written by Krase Frain

Today marks the one year anniversary of the start of the Hurlen War, or as I, the amazing reporter Krase Frain, like to refer to it as, the War of the Worlds! Hurlen seemed like an ordinary continent enough, an easy acquisition for the mighty Kobold Empire, but it seems as if fate had other things in store.

On the day of the invasion, our troops marched and easily occupied our subject’s territory, claiming it for our Empress, but then as we marched northward, crushing the weakened Hurlen Alliance, A mysterious force appeared, one only mentioned in the Mythia. The dark force of Erosia! Her armies had made a breach in this world and the forces of the night soon swept across the land, devastating everything in their wake, all who opposed her, even the dead became her minions, the husks. The husks with their so called “Husk Queen” and the Twili soldiers were a fighting force unlike anything ever before seen.

The war seemed all but lost, with the impenetrable city of Nabatea almost felled, but suddenly a report came through that Erosia had gone missing, now the war was at a standstill, and we would soon be able to make a comeback. A delegation was sent to the remnants of the Alliance, and a cease-fire was declared allowing for all our forces to focus on the Erosian front.

Today, the war has once again reached a stalemate with the borders much like it was at the beginning of the conflict. And with the Empress’s wedding around the corner, now is a perfect time for remembering all who have given their lives for the crown and for our country.

Long live the Empress! Long live Rokesh!
