Introduction:

The Following is a snapshot of the countries of Hurley at the time between the great Hurlen war and the Tri-War between the Kobold Empire (The Kledgelony), Erosia and the Twili (The Twilight Throne), and the remnants of the Hurly Federation holed up in Rus and surrounding territories.

Name: Alachua (Kingdom of Alachua)

Government: Kingdom (King Bors Madwell)

Geography: Located in the Southwestern-most edge of the continent of Hurley, bordering the Slaver’s Sea to the East, the Bay of Bliss and the Blood Sea South, the country of Mongues to the North, and Pomera to the East. the lands of Pomera are by far the most fertile on the entire continent. This fertility is thanks to the large amount of rivers that flow from the Monguian Mountain Range down through the lands of Alachua, irrigating the entire land making it 100% fertile. The land is also nice and flat permitting for ease of travel and movement of goods.

Description: Alachua was always considered to be the bread basket of the continent, it always contributed the most to the exported foodstuffs of Hurly, now contributing about 60% of all exported agricultural goods in Hurly. They originally sided with the South in the Great War, but due to a betrayal of clan Madwell, Alachua ended up causing the Southern Alliance to fall apart. This is generally considered by many historians to be the reason why the war ended when it did and why the clan now holds the throne to this day. Many of the people in the country are mad, however, as they have a king on their throne who betrayed their country, and many outsiders view the king as nothing but a puppet of Ismakale.

Name: Bliss (The Isle of Bliss)

Government: The Council of Comfort (Chief Councilor Althidon Yllaran)

Geography: Located South of Hurly between the Bay of Bliss and the Blood Sea, this Tropical Island is widely considered a paradise and is the only major landmass outside of Hurly. The temperature is fairly constant throughout the year and precipitation is semi-regular.

Description: The Isle of Bliss rarely ever engages in external affairs. Mostly keeping to themselves and making extensive profits catering to the elites of Hurly with some of the most extravagant Luxury anyone can buy. While is sided with the South in the Great War, it was mostly as a formality and did not contribute much of anything to the actual war effort.

Name: Celeste (Grand Celestial Magocracy)

Government: Magocracy (Grand Magus Enexeor)

Geography: Located to the Northwest of Ismakale, Southwest of Hossari, Southeast of Swedanetil, East of Monteforte, and North of Napolei. Along the borders with Monteforte and Napolei are the Swedcele Mountain Range. The typical midway point for travelers on their way between Ishmakale and Swedanetil. The country itself consists mainly of plains and some arable land. This country’s capital is know as the city of magic as their country is centered on developing and pushing the bounds of magic in the purest sense.

Description: This country is know as the magic casting capital of Hurly and its capital is know as the city of magic as their country is centered on developing and pushing the bounds of magic in the purest sense. The mages from this country are know continentally to be some of the best, so much so that in order to preserve this legacy, the most powerful magical magic users among them compete every 5 years to see who is the best. The winner will be crowned the Grand Magus and will rule the country like a King. After their term is up, they will become a Grand Magister, and will be one of the ones to select the next Grand Magus.

Name: Claire Fontaine (The Grand Duchy of Claire Fontaine)

Government: Grand Duchy (Grand Duchess Claire Fontaine)

Geography: Bordered in the North by Nabatea, to the East by Napolei, to the South by Mongues, and to the West by the Slaver’s Sea, Claire Fontaine is a rather mountainous country dominated by the southern Monguian Mountain Range, which contributes to much of their economy due to its rich abundance of mineral resources.

Description: Claire Fontaine is a rather new country created after a peasant revolt under then just adventurer Claire Fontaine broke out to separate the country from Napolei. After the end of the war, the people decided to elect Claire as the Duchess and she has been like a mother to the people. Thanks to her wise policies, the people of her country have become exceedingly well off and have some of the best living standards for working peasants in Hurly.

Name: Corsica (Kingdom of Corsica)

Government: Constitutional Monarchy (King Hamon)

Geography: Located South of Uterect and Fuchs, West of the Costal Sea, North of Zorat and Plana, and East of Ismakale. This land is largely dominated by the great Hurlen Plains that stretch across the Eastern Half of the Continient.

Description: This country is most known for being one of the last countries to suffer a large loss of territory to Ishmakale. Originally stretching all the way to the border with Hossari and right up against the Northern Mountain Range. Much of this territory was split between Uterect and Ishmakale and is still a sore spot for them to this day.

Name: Ete (Grand Etian Technocracy)

Government: Technocracy (Grand Innovator Binks)

Geography: Located South of Plana, West of the Papacy, North of Lakose and the Lake of Mystery, and West of Ismakale, Ete is dominated by a lush forest especially in the Southeast with its North cutting into the Great Plains.

Description: Ete is the technologically minded cousin of Celeste. Its capital boasts some of the most impressive magical artifacts and technol;ogy in the world. Its artificers are know the world over as being some of the best and brightest. The most brightest minds among them compete every year to see who can produce the best invention. The judges are a panel of independently selected previous winners who will judge the competition with a discerning eye. The winner will be crowned the Grand Innovator and will lead the Council of Ten until the end of their term. After their term is up, they will join the Council of Ten who help to govern the country.

Name: Fuchs (The Order of Fuchs)

Government: Knights Order (Grand Knight Vladimir Stalin)

Geography: Bordering the Silver Sea to the North, Rus to the Northeast, the Coastal Sea to East, Uterect to the Southwest, and Hossari to the West. Fuchs is a barren land only able to support itself thanks to its overlord of Rus. It has some forested areas, but is mainly just know to be at the foothills of the Rusan Mountians and the Northern Mountain Range.

Description: The Order of Fuchs was established after the defeat of Uterect at the hands of Rus. Wanting to not have to worry about constantly protecting their borders, Rus established this order out of the nearby Utrechtish lands. This orders’ sole job is to act as the shield for Rus so that the people of Rus can not have to bear much mind to military matters.

Name: Hossari (The Queendom of Hossari)

Government: Queendom (Queen Omylia Hossari)

Geography: Located South of the Silver Sea, West of Fuch and Uterect, North of Ishmakalale, Northeast of Celeste, and East of Swedanetil, Hossari is known for having some of the best fisheries on Hurly. The Silver Sea is brimming with fish and whales that seem nearly endless, all them right on Hossari’s footstool. The interior of the country is fairly forested and it Eastern boarder is denoted by the Northern Mountain Range.

Description: Hossari is well known for being one of the most advanced countries when it comes to military techniques and technologies. It was instrumental in the victory of the Great War and was seconded only by its much larger neighbor in Ishmakale. The Hossari, however, tend to view magic as dishonest and will shun it in society. This has lead to a few face-offs against Celeste in the past and generally has made made them especially weary of Elven mages. The Crown Princess has recently gone missing and there is a general sense of unease as to the future of the nation.

Name: Hyperion (The Eternal City of Hyperion)

Government: Republic (Lord Mayor Placus Calpurnius Sosius)

Geography: Located between Ishmakale and Pomera, Hyperion is city with a country. This city is the largest on the continent and is the center of trade, culture, learning, and religion. There is some lands East and West of the city also controlled by it which is used mainly for agriculture.

Description: Hyperion is the center of trade, culture, learning, and religion on Hurly and is widely considered the city build by Quinn and Erosia during the Era of the Children. Its existence has been around since time immamorial and never once have the walls been breached. Nearly 8 % of everyone on Hurly lives in this city alone. It sided with the North during the Great War breaking centuries of neutrality in the hopes of finally establishing peace on the continent. It now serves as the de facto capital of the Hurlen Federation.

Name: Ishmakale (Free State of Ishmakale)

Government: Monarchy (Kaiser Stefan Ishmakale)

Geography: Located at the center of the Hurly, almost half of all countries boarder Ismakale in some capacity. Ishmakale is probably the most balanced in terms of geography. The North holds the vast Ashmire Forest and the Northern Mountain Range. The center holds some fairly fertile land and rivers that eventually flow south to Pomera. The Southern and Western part is dominated by the Great Plains allowing for vast grazing opportunities.

Description: If Ishmakale were a person, they would be a jack of all trades, and a master of many. Their territory was relatively small until a few centuries ago, Ishmakale began to integrate many of the central countries into its own and through this warpath, mastered the art of war. By integrating the diverse peoples of central Hurly, they managed to create a diverse and weel-oileed political machine that optomised every asset of their country. As the leaders of the Hurlen Federation, they hope to untie the country into a similarly well processing machine so that they may go out in search of new lands to bring into the fold. As of current, they are the biggest supported of the unity mission and are financing a large portion of it, so as to establish unity on the continent.

Name: Kambot (County Kambot)

Government: County (Count Kramor Kambot)

Geography: Kambot is on the Eastern coast of Hurly and is bordered by Zorat and Plana in the North, the Costal Sea to the East, the River of Fate in the South, and the Papal States in the West. Kambot is an exceptionally swampy lands that make it fertile grounds for medicinal herbs which grow in abundance and with much variety in the Southern part of the county.

Description: Kambot is a nation of duality. While on one hand having some of the greatest experts in herbology in Hurly, those outside of this field are generally considered rednecks and somewhat uncivilized. Those who live in the swamps often being ostrocised by society in general.

Name: Lakose (The Kingdom of Lakose)

Government: Monarchy (King Diwali Lakose)

Geography: With their capital situated on the shores of the Lake of Mystery, Lakose borders the Lake, River of Fate, Ishmakale and Ete to the North, the Coastal Sea to the East, Xeries to the South, and Pomera to the West. Lakose’s geography has long been associated with the great River and Lake, often being the country that people visit if they wish to view these natural wonders. Outside of these, the Great Southern Forest dominates almost the entirety of the Eastern portion of the country and makes up a large portion of its industry, focusing on wood production.

Description: Lakose has always claimed the great waterways as part of their sole domain, but after their loss in the Great War, they were forced to cede it to be under the protection of the Federation as a whole. Despite this, Lakose still makes great efforts to publicize themselves as the premiere destination to view the place where Erosia was sealed. This has led them to be the most popular destination for the average person to visit and has the second largest tourism industry behind only the isle of Bliss.

Name: Mongues (The Mongian State)

Government: Monarchy (King Bolat Mongues)

Geography: Mongues is boarded in the North by Claire Fontaine and Napolei, in the East by Ishmakale, the South by Pomera and Alachua, and the West by the Slaver’s Sea. The Northern part of their country is dominated by the Monguian Mountain Range, whereas the South has some of exceptionally fertile land due in part to the rivers coming from the mountains.

Description: Mongues was exceptionally eager to join the war against the North in an effrot to stop Ismakalian aggression. They were allied to many of the countries that were absorbed by Ishmakale during their rise and so held many hostilities towards them. The Monguians have the largest mineral deposits in the southern part of the continent and, as such, were vital for the continued war effort for the South.

Name: Monteforte (The Mountain Fortress of Monteforte)

Government: Knightly Order (Grand Knight James Morely)

Geography: Monteforte is situated within the Swedcele Mountain Range and borders Swedanetil to the North, Celeste and Napolei to the East, Nabatea to the South, and the Slaver’s Sea to the West. Monteforte primarily consists of the Mountain Fortress situated atop Mt. Atena and is the highest altitude capital on Hurly. The Order of Moteforte have created an extensive tunnel network within the mountains allowing for ease of traversal though and ease of exploitation of the mineral resources.

Description: Monteforte was established after a mercentary company that was banished from their homelands found that the top of Mt. Atena was particularly hospitibal for human habitation. It was a magical nexus that provided much more habitable conditions than even those in the valley. The Order then set up shop and began to exploit the mountains and pressure away any one else who may wish to use the mountains for themselves. Of course, you may be permitted usage and passage, for a price. Due to their concentration on the mainland, however, Monteforte has neglected their coast which had lead to it being a breeding ground for scum and villainy.

Name: Nabatea (The Grand Republic of Nabatea)

Government: Republic (Doge Aabid el-Salah)

Geography: Located in the most arid part of Hurly, Nabatea borders Monteforte to the North, Napolei to the East, Claire Fontaine to the South, and the Slaver’s Sea to the West. Nabatea has almost no naturally producing goods due to the arid nature of the interior of their country. This has lead for much of their population to settle on the coast and focus primarily on trading as their occupation.

Description: Nabatea was hit with the sole piece of nearly entirely unusable land on the whole continent of Hurly. Despite this, the Nabatean grew strong and began to focus on trade to optomise what little advantages they could get. This lead them to becoming some of the most renowned maritime and caravaning traders on the whole of Hurly. There’s a saying on Hurly, if you are doing business involving a Nabatean, pray they are on your side.

Name: Napolei (The Kingdom of Napoei

Government: Monarchy (King Elogio Napolei)

Geography: Napolei is bordered by Celeste to the North, Ishmakale to the East, Mongues to the South, and Claire Fontaine, Nabatea, and Moteforte to the West. Napolei Is a rather poor country with the little riches that they have access to often being taken by the Government. They have access to some mountains and mineral resources, but most of their land is generally unusable.

Description: The country of Napolei is generally considered one of the most corrupt countries in the Federation. They would frequently switch sides in wars to whichever side was winning and their leaders would hoard all the wealth. The people of this country are very poor off and generally would rather be under anyone else. It is not surprise then that it has been a country under constant rebellion, the seeds of which are still making roots especially after the successful revolution of Claire Fontaine.

Name: The Papal State (The Papacy) (The Isekai’ed Holy See)

Government: Elected Theocratic Monarchy (Pope-Vicarate Victor I)

Geography: The Papacy is boarded by Plana to the North, Kambot to the East, the River of Fate and Lakose to the South, and Ete to the West. The Papal State has a dense Forest in its South with parts of the Great Plains in its North. It mainly relies on pasturization and timber as its primary exports.

Description: The Papacy was founded when a man by the name of Bishop Barron on day appeared in the area the is now the Papcy. He claimed that he was from another world and that he followed a religion called “Catholicism”. He began to spread this faith and created a large following in the area that is now the Papacy. The ruler of the land even converted and turned over his country to this Bishop. When praying about what he should do, a vision came the Bishop of his God that told him to bring himself and the first eleven priests that he ordained to the spot where he first arrived, where there was now a large cathedral. Once all 12 had gathered, the heavens opened up and what appeared to Jesus and Mary and the choirs of angels appeared to the Bishop and his priests and told Bishop Barron that he was now called Surculus and that he and his successors would be the branch of God’s Church on Phantos. Bishop Barron then became Pope-Vicarate Surculus and his 11 priests became Bishops. Since then, Pope Surculus has died and Bishop Qildor was elected to be the next Pope and took the name of Victor. While the followers of this new religion are few, their numbers are slowly spreading and growing throughout the continent.

Name: Plana (The Planan Nomads)

Government: Nomadic Tribes (Chief Yegu af Teleboge)

Geography: The region of Plana is bordered by Corsica to the North, Zorat to the East, Ete, the Papacy, and Kambot to the South, and Ishmakale to the West. The region is dominated by the Great Plains ideal for Nomadic pasturalism, the dominant trade of its denizens. While the majority are nomads, the capital city is set is a fixed location and is where many of the cultural event occur for the Planan people.

Description: Plana has always been a difficult area to govern as the vast plains that cover it have always made nomadism the only real way to make use of the lands. Despite this, many governments have been slowly encroaching on Planan lands and have pushed them is to the small region which the now control, less than half their original size. The Planan people generally either are left to accept this or move, with many ending up becoming assimilated into the new lands of their invaders.

Name: Pomera (The People Republic of Pomera)

Government: Dictatorial Republic (Consuls Arturo Valdovinos and Luka Fernández)

Geography: Borders Ishmakale to the North, Lakose to teh East, the Bay of Bliss, to the South, and Mongues and Alachua to the West. The lands of Pomera are ideal for farming and its access to the Bay of Bliss makes it an ideal fishing country as well. The Pomerans specialize in cash crops like cotton and tobacco that are exploited to the rest of the continent.

Description: Pomera was the most active fighter in the Great War, bearing the brunt of enemy fores and attempting to maintain their independence from the North. Pomerans generally view the Federation as just another way of Ishmakale expanding their boundaries. Generally, the Pomerans are against the idea of the Federation and generally wish to leave. The current Consuls were put in place by the peace treaty, but many of the Federation fear theat after the next election, the Pomerans may once agin return to their antagonistic ways.

Name: Rus (The Rus Kingdom)

Government: Monarchy (King Bors IX)

Geography: Located in the far Northeast, bordering Fuchs to the Southwest, the Silver Sea to the West, and the Costal Sea to the Southeast.Rus is generally considered a Mountainous Frozen wasteland with the Rusan Mountains Dominating the majority of the country, but for the Rus, who live inside the Mountains, this is not a problem. The Rus make use of the high volcanic activity to produce fantastic metallurgy and Runic enhancements. This has earned them the moniker of “The Frozen Forge.”

Description: Rus has generally held a “Not my circus, not my monkeys” approach to the rest of Hurly, staying out of almost all external affairs. Their primary focus has always been on developing their metallurgy and trading for the foodstuffs to sustain their population. The vassel state of Fuchs was established to basically keep out any would-be invaders, not that anyone would have a chance to successfully invade. Rus has had the oldest boarders of anyone else on the continent and, if they have their way, they alway will.

Name: Swedanetil (Kingdom of Swedanetil)

Government: Monarchy (Queen-Regent Aibhilín Uí Maonghaile)

Geography: Located on the Northwestern coast of Hurly, it borders The Silver Sea to the North, Hossari to the East, Celeste to the Southeast, Monteforte to the South, and the Slaver’s Sea to the West. Swedanetil has a vast coast that is ideal for fishing while also having a vast forest in the interior of the country and the Swedcele Mountain Range denoting its Southenrn Boarder.

Description: Swedancele is generally considered a relaxed country that focuses on developing their industries and avoiding too much war. They rely primarily on diplomatic ties to ensure thier peace and security.

Name: Utrecht (The Plurinational State of Utrecht)

Government: Oligarchy (President Bolivié Utrecht)

Geography: Bordering Fuchs to the Northeast, Corsica to the South, Ishmakale to the Southwest, and Hossari to the West, Utrecht is largely forested with the Northern Mountain Range denoting its Western boarder.

Description: Utrecht generally is considered one of the more greedy nations, taking much land from its neighbours throughout its time, they finally bit off more than they could chew when they attempted to capture the Rusan Kingdom. This war casued them to loose their coast and has embittered them ever since. They still maintain a standing fleet despite the lack of a port as a way to show that it still claims its rightful coast.

Name: Xeries (The Kingdom of Xeries)

Government: Monarchy (King Tobias Xeries)

Geography: Bordered by Lakose to the North and East, The Blood Sea to the South, and Pomera to the Northwest. Xeries’ east consists of a large forested area and its west is fairly suitable to farming. It has a high access to fishing thanks to its access to the Blood Sea, which has a large population of skeel, a delicacy in seafood.

Description: Xeries has been one of the few countries that has had a large standing navy, as it is needed to hunt the skeel, large carnivorous fish that have the ability to cast electric magic. Xeries used this fleet during the Great War to defend the South against the Navies of the North. At the end of the War, their navy was taken to be integrated into the larger Federation navy.

Name: Zorat (The United Tribes of Zorat)

Government: Democratic-Republic (President Igtos Drank)

Geography: Zorat is bordered by Corsica to the North, the Costal Sea to the East, Kambot in the South, and Plana to the West. Zorat’s west has a portion of the Great Plains and its west has a few forested areas. Zorat has very few major economic opportunities as most people find the Goblins untrustworthy.

Description: Zorat is a despicable country of Goblins that holds an election every 4 years for the president of their country, who shares their power with other branches of government. The first of these other two is the Zorat Legislator with two houses: the upper house that sends two representatives from each tribe, the elder and one elected; the lower house sends political party representatives based on percentage of the popular vote. The last branch of its governance is the Supreme Court where 7 judges judge the most important cases of the country. This has caused all sorts of corruption from all that vying for power and the idiocracy of their peasants. Everyone knows that peasants cannot possibly know about the intricacies of politics.
