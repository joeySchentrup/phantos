The Story of the Two Children

Our Story begins at the dawn of time for this little planet, when all that roamed here where the six primordial dragons-Ouro'ras, Rokesh, Yvander, Vlaurunga, Quintara Lotus, and Golestandt-, who where given this world by Kallisto, the creator. The dragons soon grew lonely however, and thus used their powerful magic to bring life into this world. Rokesh used his Earth magic to bring to life all the beasts and wild creatures of the land. Yvander used her Ice magic to create all the creature of the sea. Next, Quintara Lotus used her Arcane magic to create all the creatures born of magic. Then Vlaurunga used her Fire magic to create the creatures of metal and machine. Finally Ouro'ras with his Light magic created the sentient people of the world and Golestandt with his Dark magic created all the creatures of the night. After they had finished this, the primordial six were tired and decided that they should use their powers to create two final beings, one to rule the day and one to rule the night. These two were named Quint, day, and Erosia, night.

Many years past as this continued to unfold. The dragons were pleased with their work and remained out of the affairs of their creations and begun to entertain each other. Quint and Erosia got along well, but Quint used his power excessively, almost as if he thought himself a fool. Meanwhile, the people all praised him, for they were of the day and not the night. As time grew on, Erosia grew jealous of her brother and plotted to overthrow him and take his power. She convinced the Twilight Elves, or the Twili, to join her in her mission.

Then, one day it happened. Erosia used her power to maintain the night and allowed the Twili to conquer the people of the day. Quint tried to stop this, but while Quint was fooling around with his powers, Erosia was honing hers. Erosia quickly beat Quint and he was forced to flee. Quint went to the dragons for help and they gave him this: “Amongst our creation shall be born those who are destined to be our champions and shall help you in your struggle, one who represents each of us.” And so Quint went to find the champions. Once he had, they went to face Erosia together. With their combined force, they managed to defeat Erosia and banish her and her Twili to the Twilight, where it would be near impossible to escape. Quint became th one ruler of both day and night and the champions were rewarded for their service.

But lo, a prophecy was foretold with Erosia’s defeat: “The one who was cast out from this world shall return when one of the light who follows the dark lights the bridge for darkness to return. Fear not though! For just as the champions battled Erosia before so too shall champions be born anew to face the foe who threatens all.”

-Miathra 5
